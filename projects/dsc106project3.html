Global Temperature Change Across Emission Scenarios

  • Historical — Real-world climate data from 1850–2014. This is the baseline used to understand how temperatures have changed before future projections begin.
  • SSP245 — A “middle‑of‑the‑road” future scenario. Society continues current trends with moderate climate policies.
  • SSP585 — A high‑emissions, fossil‑fuel‑intensive future. Rapid economic growth and heavy energy use lead to strong warming.

Data source:  CMIP 6
